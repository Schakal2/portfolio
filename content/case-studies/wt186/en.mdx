## Overview & Concept

WT-186 is a native audio plug-in built to explore **Wave Terrain Synthesis** — an uncommon synthesis method where a virtual particle moves across a three-dimensional surface, and its spatial coordinates dictate the generated waveform. 

The project bridges complex digital signal processing with an intuitive, hardware-inspired workflow, allowing users to generate terrain data natively from uploaded audio and image files.

- **UI/UX Design**: Figma, custom vector components, interactive LCD radar feedback loop.

## Key Technical Challenges & Solutions

### 1. Managing Chaos in Wave Terrain Synthesis
* **The Challenge**: Wave terrain algorithms inherently generate extreme high-frequency noise when the scanning radius samples vast height arrays within a single cycle.
* **The Solution**: Implemented precise scaling controls, splitting parameters into coarse and fine adjustments, and designed an integrated macro and modulation matrix to give producers quick tactile control over chaotic soundscapes.

![](/images/wt186/23.png)
![](/images/wt186/3.png)

---

### 2. Hardware-Informed FX Architecture (Roland Jupiter-4)
* **The Challenge**: Replicating the distinct character of vintage hardware without relying on vague "analog warmth" tropes.
* **The Solution**: Researched the circuit architecture of the **Roland Jupiter-4**. Identified a specific pre-filter circuit featuring a sub-oscillator and an ensemble unit incorporating a low-pass filter, and successfully integrated this topology into the plug-in's FX chain.

![](/images/wt186/vid1.gif)
![](/images/wt186/vid2.gif)
![](/images/wt186/vid3.gif)

---

### 3. Streamlining the Modulation Matrix & UI Hierarchy
* **The Challenge**: Mod matrices in software synths often suffer from cluttered interfaces and steep learning curves.
* **The Solution**: Designed a space-saving layout by grouping modulation controls directly adjacent to modulators within the matrix. Visual feedback, such as a draggable crosshair on an LCD radar display, was prioritized to instantly communicate the particle's spatial orbit.

## Product Roadmap & Next Steps

1. **In-Depth User Testing**: Conducting beta testing sessions and small synth jams to refine parameter ranges and ensure the instrument feels intuitive for producers.
2. **UI Refinement**: De-cluttering visual hierarchies by separating the FX low-pass filter from the global filter and elevating the central Morph knob for seamless real-time wavetable transitions.
3. **Preset Library**: Finalizing default presets utilizing unique geographical height data, such as Mount Everest topographical maps, to showcase the engine's textural range.
