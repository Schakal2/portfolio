## Background & Problem Statement

<Callout>
Imagine this: You sit in a crowded waiting room at your local government office for 2 hours. When your number is finally called, you are told at the counter in 30 seconds: *“Proof of income is missing on page 4, please book another appointment.”*
</Callout>

In Germany, public offices are legally required under the Online Access Act (OZG) to provide digital service applications. However, the reality often falls short: citizens submit documents via email or post and hear nothing for weeks.

The result? Persistent anxiety for applicants, confusion over missing paperwork, and overloaded phone helplines at public offices handling nervous status check calls.

## Discovery: 30 Survey Responses & 4 In-Depth Interviews

To avoid building in a vacuum, we surveyed 30 citizens and conducted 4 intensive user interviews. From this data, we crafted 2 **User Personas** and a **User Journey Map** revealing peak points of frustration.

  <StatCard number="30" label="Citizens surveyed in quantitative research" />
  <StatCard number="4" label="In-depth user interviews analyzing pain points" />
  <StatCard number="2" label="User Personas & 1 comprehensive User Journey Map" />
</StatGrid>

![](/images/ebehoerde/SurveyEN.png)

### Core Citizen Demands:
1. **“Just tell me where my application is.”** (Real-time tracking timeline)
2. **“Help me organize my paperwork.”** (Clear division into *pending* vs *completed*)
3. **“Don't torture me with 10-page PDFs.”** (Answering only relevant questions)

## From Confusion to Clarity: User Flow & Prototyping

With the User Journey in place, we mapped the **User Flow** and iterated across **Lo-Fi** and **Hi-Fi prototypes**.

![](/images/ebehoerde/UserFlowEN.png)
![](/images/ebehoerde/HIFI1EN.png)

### 3 Key Screens

#### 1. Dashboard: Application Tracker
Instead of worrying for weeks, the visual timeline shows current progress (*Submitted → Under Review → Approved*). Immediate notifications flag missing documents.

---

#### 2. Digital Document Vault (Family File)
Never lose track of uploaded paperwork. The vault separates **pending items** from **completed procedure archives**.

---

#### 3. Smart Forms: 5 Pages of Bureaucracy Bypassed
An interactive questionnaire replaces static PDFs, using logic jumps to bypass irrelevant sections automatically.

## Explorations & Decisions: Color & Form Architecture

<Exploration title="Color Palette 1: German Flag Colors (Black, Red, Gold)" selected={false}>
Felt outdated, visually overwhelming, and lacked accessible contrast for visually impaired users.
</Exploration>

<Exploration title="Color Palette 2: Accessible Blue & Slate Gray (WCAG Compliant)" selected={true}>
Meets high accessibility standards (WCAG), conveys trust, and guarantees optimal legibility.
</Exploration>

<Exploration title="Forms 1: Digitizing static paper PDFs 1:1" selected={false}>
Does not solve the root issue: language remains cryptic and non-applicable fields still confuse users.
</Exploration>

<Exploration title="Forms 2: Dynamic step-by-step logic + application timeline" selected={true}>
Removes 90% of user anxiety. Pending actions are immediately clear and completed records safely archived.
</Exploration>

## Key Takeaways

<Callout>
**Structured organization & accessible design remove anxiety from bureaucracy.**
When an interface clearly separates *pending* from *completed* tasks, embraces accessibility, and communicates status transparently, the process remains simple — regardless of how complex the underlying public service is.
</Callout>
